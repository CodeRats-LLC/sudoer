## 0.1.0-alpha.2 — 2026-10-08

- **Your account on the web.** Sign in at
  [coderats.llc/account](https://coderats.llc/account) with GitHub or
  Google to manage billing — card, invoices, plan changes — through
  the customer portal.
- **Fixes.** Signing up for news on coderats.llc now sticks; earlier
  signups could be lost when the server restarted.
- **Updates prove themselves.** If you installed alpha.1, this is the
  release it offers you — tell us if it didn't.

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 — 2026-10-08

The first public alpha of Sudoer — the opening of a long campaign
toward a new way to work.

- **One window to start.** Sign in with GitHub or Google from the
  launch screen; no card, no setup prompts.
- **15-day free trial** with everything on. Subscribe from inside the
  app when you are ready.
- **Updates itself.** New releases are offered at launch or within
  the hour.
- **Tell us what's missing.** Decline at the end of the trial and
  Sudoer asks — not forces — how it could be better.

Known: alpha builds are signed but not notarized, so a downloaded copy
needs "Open Anyway" once (the install script does not).
